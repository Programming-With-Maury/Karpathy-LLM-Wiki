# What Should The Alpha Prioritize?

## Summary

The alpha should prioritize local ingest, domain classification, source-backed page updates, and revision visibility.

## Answer

Based on the [launch plan](../sources/product-os-launch-plan.md), the highest-value alpha workflow is: upload messy source material, classify it into a domain, update a small set of source-backed pages, and expose what changed through revisions.
