# Acme Product OS

## Summary

Acme Product OS is the fictional demo product used to show how a domain-aware LLM wiki can preserve context for AI agents.

## Key Points

- It targets small AI-native teams.
- It emphasizes local-first knowledge management.
- It treats source traceability as a core product requirement.
- It separates raw evidence from curated synthesis so agents can inspect both layers.
- It uses domain routing to avoid mixing unrelated product, research, and customer feedback material.

## Evidence / Notes

- The [launch plan](../sources/product-os-launch-plan.md) frames domain separation as a launch requirement.
- The [customer feedback notes](../../customer-feedback/sources/customer-feedback-pilot-notes.md) show why persistent project context matters to pilot users.
